## Name

ROSARY

## Short description

Pray the rosary on your wrist, bead by bead, with a gentle buzz at every Hail Mary.

## Description

ROSARY turns your watch into a rosary. A ring of eleven beads runs around the edge of the screen: one Our Father and ten Hail Marys. The bead you are praying always sits at the bottom and pulses softly; the beads already prayed are filled in, the ones still ahead are outlined. The centre shows which decade you are on (1 / 5 to 5 / 5) and names the current prayer.

Moving on is one touch away: tap anywhere on the screen, swipe up, down or left, or press the Select or Down button, and the ring turns to the next bead. You can pray without looking: a short buzz marks every Hail Mary, a light tick tells you the decade is over, and a double buzz announces the next one.

After the tenth Hail Mary the ring dims and a cross appears with the closing prayers, Glory Be and O my Jesus. Pray them at your own pace, then tap to move on to the next decade. After the fifth decade the app tells you the rosary is complete, and the next tap starts a new one. To start over at any time, press Up twice.

Your progress is kept on the watch, so you can leave the app and come back to the same bead. The screen stays on while you pray. No account, no phone needed, and it works fully offline.
