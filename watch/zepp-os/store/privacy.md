# ROSARY privacy statement

ROSARY (Zepp OS watch app by motyl.dev) collects no personal data and shares no data with anyone.

- **No network access.** The app never connects to the internet, the phone or any server. It
  works fully offline and needs no account.
- **Progress stays on the watch.** The only thing the app saves is where you are in the rosary
  (current decade and bead), in the watch's local storage. It never leaves the watch and goes
  with the app: uninstalling ROSARY removes it.
- **Device information.** The app reads the device info only to get the screen size, so the
  bead ring fits the display. Nothing else is read and nothing is stored or sent.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.**

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/gmotyl/rosary
